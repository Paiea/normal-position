## Culture gate

Before every chapter ask:

- what household labor is actually happening?
- which structure is being used and why?
- does weather alter work?
- does kinship obligation change a choice?
- who has practical access versus moral permission?
- is any cultural detail merely decorative?

If the chapter could occur unchanged in a generic American haunted house:
repair it.

## Normal baseline gate

Before using an object as horror:

- has its normal state been established?
- who knows that normal?
- is the reader entitled to recognize the deviation?
- is there a human path for the change?
- would not changing it be stronger?

Do not create horror merely by pointing the prose camera at an ordinary object.
