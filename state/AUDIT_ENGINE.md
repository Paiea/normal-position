# NORMAL POSITION — AUDIT ENGINE

Use RED / AMBER / GREEN.

RED blocks publication.

## 1. Canon

Check:
- time
- location
- weather
- object position
- injuries
- family knowledge
- Lea-history knowledge
- supernatural rules
- compound routes

Contradiction with published prose is RED.

## 2. Object continuity

Use OBJECT_LEDGER.

Check:
- normal established before meaningful deviation
- movement has plausible path unless confirmed supernatural
- owner / keeper / touch rule coherent
- object not suspicious only because narration spotlights it
- ordinary objects remain ordinary
- Mara's knowledge comes from real expertise
- other expertise matters
- interpretation changes without rewriting observation

Decisive continuity failure is RED.

## 3. Horror

Check:
- dread arises from known normal
- no unnecessary new phenomenon
- human explanation remains available when useful
- false negatives still exist
- horror changes relationship or interpretation, not just atmosphere
- supernatural knowledge creates pressure rather than convenience
- house is not personified beyond evidence

Escalation by random novelty is AMBER.
New power invented to solve scene is RED.

## 4. Culture

Check:
- architecture affects action
- weather affects labor
- kin obligation affects decision
- privacy vs shared responsibility is active
- culture is not decorative vocabulary
- no fake authentic folklore
- no island mysticism stereotype

## 5. Character independence

Across rolling 4 chapters:
- does Tomas have work?
- does Inez have obligation beyond haunting?
- does Celina pursue estate / departure goals?
- does Lio have a current life?
- is everybody merely reacting to Mara?

Pure satellite drift is RED for major characters.

## 6. Voice

Search for house-style convergence:
- clipped categorical debate
- repeated Good / No / Of course / There
- thesis line after action
- identical joke rhythm

Remove tags mentally.
Can speakers still be distinguished?

## 7. Nonverbal

Check:
- room has bodies and objects
- dialogue is not floating
- gestures are specific
- changed object use can replace explanation
- no nod/sigh/smile filler epidemic

## 8. Lea handling

Lea must remain a child who lived, not horror decoration.

Check:
- memories include preferences / irritation / play
- her death does not become the only fact about her
- no creepy-child shorthand
- adult characters' guilt does not erase her personhood

## 9. Reader model

Write privately:
- what does reader think is happening?
- what human explanation remains plausible?
- what supernatural model is strongest?
- what fact could change either?

Confusion caused by missing basic information is not dread.

## 10. Chapter necessity

Chapter must change at least one:
- knowledge
- relationship
- access
- object normal
- trust
- family history
- supernatural model
- plan
- risk

Atmosphere-only chapters need exceptional justification.

## 11. Explanation audit

After every strong physical beat ask:
did the next paragraph restate it?

Cut when action already communicates.

## 12. Midbook accumulation

Every 4 chapters:
- repeated scene engines?
- too many object anomalies?
- too few ordinary tasks?
- independent life disappearing?
- chapter lengths collapsing into serial fragments?
- culture fading?
- horror becoming lore?

Repair future contracts immediately.
