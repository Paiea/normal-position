# NORMAL POSITION — CHARACTER SYSTEM

Status: ACTIVE.

## Core model

Every recurring character gets three separate channels:

### BODY
What they physically do.

### VOICE
What they are willing to say.

### PRIVATE INTERPRETATION
What they think is happening.

These should often disagree.

## Object behavior is character behavior

For each major character track:
- what they maintain
- what they lose
- what they borrow
- what they never touch
- what they repair
- what they discard
- whether they notice placement
- whether they return things
- what they treat as communal
- what they treat as private

Do not assign quirks merely to differentiate cast.

Promote repeated behavior after rehearsal.

## Independent-life rule

Every major character needs:
- work
- obligation
- relationship outside Mara
- problem unrelated to the haunting
- competence Mara does not own
- something Mara finds boring

If Mara vanished for a week, each major character must still have something to do.

## Frame-breaker

Working frame-breaker:
**Tomas Orlan**.

He understands practical island systems Mara does not:
- engines
- boats
- storm preparation
- local material repair
- gear storage
- weather timing

He does not exist to correct her thematically.

He should sometimes be right about an object or structure for boring professional reasons.

## Present-tense danger

At least one loved / understandable character must make a current choice Mara would not make and refuse to retract it.

Working candidate:
**Inez**.

Her care for Adela and the compound must have sharpened into a willingness to use invasive household knowledge for protection / control.

Do not make her secretly evil.

## Voice audit

Remove names and tags.

Characters should differ through:
- what question they answer
- what they ignore
- how much context they provide
- whether they apologize
- whether they touch an object while talking
- whether they keep working
- whether they interpret silence
- what kind of evidence convinces them
- what they consider private

Do not let everyone become a concise clever debater.
