# NOHEA KEALOHA — CHILDHOOD CHARACTER CARD

Status: LOCKED ENOUGH FOR CHAPTERS 10-15.

Age at death:
7.

Relationship:
Malia's younger sister.

## Prime rule

Nohea was a child who lived before she became a family wound.

Do not write her as:
- eerie
- unusually wise
- saintly
- prophetic
- a supernatural child
- a symbol of innocence

She was seven.

## Temperament

Working traits supported by existing manuscript / family history:

- stubborn
- funny when cornered
- willing to argue past usefulness
- physically bold in small ways
- dislikes being told an answer is final
- steals Malia's things when she thinks Malia's version is better
- capable of hiding something after an argument and then caring enough to retrieve it later
- quick to deny obvious guilt
- willing to bribe Malia afterward rather than apologize cleanly

## Existing ordinary memories

### The yellow cup

Nohea used a yellow cup with painted flowers.

She insisted one flower looked like a chicken.

Malia argued it was a bird.

This argument lasted longer than the subject deserved.

### Malia's cup

Nohea repeatedly stole Malia's cup because she claimed water tasted colder from it.

She once denied doing so with water still on her upper lip.

She later bribed Malia with half a li hing mui candy that had collected pocket lint.

### Reaching

Nohea climbed onto a kitchen chair to reach high cups.

Malia warned she would fall.

Nohea answered:
"Then catch me."

This is ordinary sibling behavior, not foreshadowing.

## Locked pre-storm object

Small painted tin whistle.

No supernatural significance.

On the day of the fatal storm:
- Nohea hides the whistle in the detached cookhouse after an argument with Malia
- later she slips out during a temporary lull to retrieve it
- this choice helps place her physically outside when conditions worsen

Do not make the whistle call, sound by itself, return, or become the cause of the haunting.

## Malia relationship

Malia was ten.

Dynamic:
- older-sister authority without adult power
- reporting on Nohea
- catching / warning / correcting
- annoyance
- loyalty
- possession disputes
- bargaining
- occasional superiority
- real affection not constantly spoken

Important:
Malia's guilt should not become:
"I caused Nohea's death because we argued."

The argument / whistle explains behavior.
It does not transfer adult responsibility onto ten-year-old Malia.

## Nalani relationship

Mother.

Nohea could push Nalani differently from Malia:
- more direct refusal
- quicker physical affection after conflict
- less investment in proving she was technically right

Do not idealize.

## Tūtū Lehua relationship

Lehua's order constrained Nohea:
- cups high
- asking before access
- household rules
- storm procedure

Nohea tested rules more openly than Malia.

Important:
this does not make Nohea a freedom-symbol and Malia a conformity-symbol.

Both children adapted differently.

## Kalei relationship

Aunt / household adult.

Working use:
Kalei remembers practical incidents Malia does not because Kalei was older and present around chores.

Do not make Kalei the exclusive keeper of Nohea truth.

## Keoni relationship

Keoni was 13 at Nohea's death.

He was old enough to find both girls irritating and young enough to be inside the same child / cousin world.

Before Chapter 15, seed at least one Keoni memory of Nohea that has nothing to do with the storm.

Good lane:
Nohea touching / borrowing / interfering with something he was trying to build or repair.

## Object behavior

Nohea:
- borrowed without asking
- moved cups
- hid objects when angry
- returned things imperfectly
- cared about personal objects but not reverently
- used household furniture as access equipment
- may leave evidence of use because she was seven

This matters because an arrangement associated with Nohea should feel like **a child's lived use**, not a memorial display.

## Voice memory

Do not create a polished catchphrase.

Her remembered speech may be:
- literal when useful
- strategically unreasonable
- confident about nonsense
- quick to change argument instead of concede

Keep remembered lines short.

## Physical memory

Useful:
- bare feet on chair seat
- wet upper lip after stealing water
- pockets full of junk / candy
- reaching before asking
- taking the wrong thing because it is closer
- leaving something where an adult would not

Avoid:
- white dress
- standing silently in doorways
- humming
- uncanny staring
- horror-child imagery

## Story function

Nohea's old "place" in the household matters because she once occupied it repeatedly.

The family place may later reconstruct:
- her cup side
- sleeping edge
- small storage position
- routes
- access patterns

The story must never imply:
Nohea wants this.

Arrangement is not desire.

Memory is not consent.

## Before preference phase

Chapter 10:
Nohea may be absent entirely or appear only in one ordinary Keoni memory.

Chapters 11-14:
if Nohea-linked arrangement appears, balance it with at least one detail proving she was a messy, particular kid rather than a sacred absence.

Chapter 15:
comb confirmation belongs to Lehua's system, not Nohea's ghost.
