# NORMAL POSITION — FAMILY HISTORY LOCK

Status: LOCKED ENOUGH FOR BOOK ONE DRAFTING.

This file contains author knowledge.
Characters do not automatically know all of it.

## The Lea storm

Twenty-four years before Chapter One:

- Mara is 10.
- Lea Orlan, Mara's younger sister, is 7.
- A severe island storm is already underway.
- Adela runs the household's storm procedure with extreme precision.
- Celina and her daughters are staying at the compound.
- Inez and young Tomas are also present.

### The immediate problem

Earlier that day, Lea hides a small personal item in the detached kitchen house after an argument with Mara.

Working object:
a cheap painted tin whistle.

The whistle itself is NOT supernatural and must not become a cursed-object solution.

After the main house is secured for the worst weather, Lea slips out during a temporary lull to retrieve it.

She does not tell Mara.

### Adela's choice

Adela realizes Lea is outside before the fatal moment.

Opening the main storm door during the strongest gust cycle creates a real risk:
- pressure on the door
- flying debris
- loss of the secure interior
- danger to everyone clustered inside

Adela chooses to wait for the next lull rather than open immediately.

This choice is not obviously irrational.

It is also not cleanly defensible.

The detached kitchen structure partially fails before the lull.

Lea is fatally injured.

### What Mara remembers

Mara remembers:
- Lea missing
- adults shouting
- the storm
- her mother trying to move toward the door
- Adela stopping somebody
- the sound of something striking the outer wall
- later being told Lea had slipped out and nobody knew until too late

Mara does NOT remember hearing Adela say she knew Lea was outside.

She was a child under extreme stress.

Do not manufacture repressed-memory melodrama.
Her memory is incomplete, not magically blocked.

### What Celina knows

Celina knows Adela understood Lea was outside before the kitchen failed.

Celina tried to open the door.

Adela stopped her.

Celina has spent decades treating leaving the island as the only morally survivable response.

She has never forgiven Adela.

She has also never told Mara the full sequence because:
- Mara was a child
- Celina feared turning Mara's grief into hatred
- Celina did not want to relive her own failure to overpower Adela
- silence became easier each year

### What Inez knows

Inez also knows.

Her interpretation differs.

She believes opening at that exact moment might have killed more people.

She does not think Adela was right in some abstract moral sense.

She thinks Adela made an impossible storm decision and then carried it badly.

Inez's loyalty to Adela includes anger at Celina for converting one terrible choice into a total judgment of their mother.

This disagreement is real.

The book must not announce a single morally correct reading.

### What Tomas knows

Tomas was 13.

He knows more than Mara and less than Celina / Inez.

He remembers:
- Adela controlling the door
- Celina fighting her
- the timing of the gusts
- helping after the kitchen failed

His later mechanical / weather competence changes how he interprets the risk.

He is NOT automatic proof that Inez is right.

### What Adela did afterward

Adela simplified the family story.

She allowed:
"Lea slipped outside in the storm and we realized too late."

She did not publicly confess:
"I knew she was outside and chose to wait."

After Lea's death, Adela aggressively reorganized the compound:
- Lea's sleeping place removed
- child belongings boxed
- cup reassigned / removed
- routes and chores changed
- kitchen rebuilt / repaired
- storm rules tightened
- normal positions standardized even more strongly

This is when Mara's childhood training in exact placement became especially intense.

### Why the haunting reaches backward

The supernatural pattern is NOT "Lea wants revenge."

The compound's returning arrangements gradually reach toward the last deeply repeated household configuration in which Lea still occupied a place.

Adela's later decades of forced order sit on top of that older layer.

This produces conflicting normals.

The horror is partly that the house has no ethical distinction between:
- care
- grief
- surveillance
- routine
- assigned role

It remembers arrangement.

## Present-tense mirror

Inez must eventually make a current storm / access decision that echoes Adela without duplicating it.

Locked function:
Inez restricts another family member's movement because she believes immediate freedom creates greater collective danger.

Mara understands the reasoning.

Mara still rejects the unilateral choice.

Inez does not immediately retract it or apologize it away.

Exact scene remains flexible until Act III rehearsal.
