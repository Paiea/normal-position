# NORMAL POSITION — INHERITED LESSONS

Status: ACTIVE CRAFT MEMORY.

Inherited from Do Not Knock and When the Pictures Breathe.
Lessons travel.
Canon does not.

## 1. Premise must become human pressure

The supernatural mechanism must create:
- boundaries
- choices
- unequal access
- reasons to trust / distrust
- costs
- consequences

A cool return of an object is not enough.

Ask:
who can now accuse whom, control whom, protect whom, or invade whom because this happened?

## 2. Culture changes causality

Culture must affect:
- house layout
- labor
- privacy
- inheritance
- hospitality
- mourning
- weather preparation
- who may touch what

No decorative ethnography.

## 3. Build independent people early

Major characters need lives Mara finds boring.

Do not invent lateral lives in Act III after everyone has become a haunting satellite.

## 4. Strengths become expensive

Mara's competence:
restore a room to standard.

Expensive version:
she assumes restoring normal is care.

Use strengths past their moral safe limit before inventing random flaws.

## 5. Repetition changes meaning

Repeat:
- keys
- shutters
- cups
- routes
- evening resets
- storm checks

Do not constantly invent new scares.

## 6. Present-tense danger

A morally complicated person must make a current choice whose logic is understandable and still unacceptable to another loved character.

Inez carries this requirement.

## 7. Rehearsal discovers behavior

Freeze.
Cast.
Perform.
Compare.
Steal only what survives.

Protect truth and consequence.
Do not protect favorite dialogue.

## 8. Body / voice / private interpretation

Keep these distinct.

Thought is not dialogue captions.
Bodies can answer.

## 9. Objects carry explanation

Before narrating:
"she no longer trusted her,"
ask whether she:
- moves the key
- closes the drawer
- changes cup
- keeps her bag
- sleeps elsewhere

## 10. Stop explaining after the scene lands

Do not translate every physical beat into theme.

Trust the reader.

## 11. Middle-book lived time

Do not stack tiny concept episodes.

Use:
- repair
- food
- weather
- visitors
- work
- cleanup
- sleep
- travel
- awkward aftermath

Add life, not lore.

## 12. Outside competence

Mara cannot monopolize interpretation.

Tomas, Inez, Celina, and Lio each know different normal states.

## 13. Climax synthesizes known mechanics

No banishing spell discovered in Chapter 29.

Use:
- routes
- ownership
- repeated labor
- architecture
- weather
- learned object rules

## 14. Shared maintenance beats rescue

Ending should create ongoing work and a new lived household pattern.

## 15. Audio matters

Speaker ownership, rhythm, action ownership, and thought/dialogue difference must survive TTS.

## 16. Protect tonal difference

DNK:
threshold intimacy.

WTPB:
craft and personhood.

NO PROPS:
forensic precision.

NORMAL POSITION:
domestic familiarity becoming coercive.

Do not let shared process create one house style.
